# Audio Subsystem Directory
This directory houses audio definitions, sound effects, ambient stems, and radio broadcast playlists:
- `audio/ambiance/`: Field sound recordings of Dhaka city streets, tea stalls, traffic horns, crows, riverboats.
- `audio/vehicles/`: Engine sounds for Cycle Rickshaws, CNG 2-strokes, diesel buses, and horns.
- `audio/combat/`: Firearm reports, melee impacts, reload SFX.
- `audio/radio/`: Music streams for in-game radio stations (Radio Padma, Dhaka Beats, Surma Folk, Kotha FM).
- `audio/voice/`: Localized Bengali and English voice acting lines.
