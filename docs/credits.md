### CORE OPEN-WORLD SYSTEMS DELIVERED
1. **Foundation & Character Locomotion**: 8-way directional locomotion, sprinting, stamina recovery, stair step-up, coyote-time jump buffering, hard landing impact recovery, aiming reticle, third-person spring-arm dual-pivot camera.
2. **Advanced Vehicle Simulation**: Realistic Ackermann steering, automatic 6-speed transmission with reverse, torque curves, engine braking, tire drift smoke, procedural combustion loops, directional sirens, horn audio, safe driver mounting/dismounting.
3. **Living Traffic & Pedestrian Simulation**: Roadway spline graphs, priority intersections, automated 3-phase traffic lights, obstacle raycast braking, crowd flocking, panicked pedestrian fleeing, authentic vehicle fleet (Sedan, Rickshaw, CNG Auto-Rickshaw, Local Bus, SUV, Motorcycle).
4. **Police Heat & Tactical Response System**: 5-Star heat escalation, dynamic vision cone raycasting with day/night sensory sensitivity, non-teleporting police patrol cars, roadblock checkpoints with tire-popping spike strips, tactical radar HUD, evasion countdown timer, and interactive Thana (police station).
5. **Original Bangladesh Story & Narrative Engine**: Non-derivative delta crime narrative centered around protagonist Joynal "Joy" Abedin, sister Sumaiya, mentor Kabir Mollah, journalist Nila Chowdhury, and antagonist Chairman Afsarul "Balu" Huq. 3 complete playable story missions with cinematic dual-language letterbox dialogue and waypoint beacons.
6. **In-Game Economy & Commerce**: Wallet cash and digital bank balance (BDT ৳), 24/7 Rupali Bank ATMs, Mama Tong tea stalls (consumable food/drink with buff timers), Bismillah General Store, Bhai Bhai Auto Workshop (repairs, custom colors, engine tuning, bullbar armor), Lalbagh Tailors (5 authentic outfits), residential safehouses with sleep saving, 16-slot inventory, and 3D physical world pickups.
7. **Expanded 7-Region Open World**: 512m modular streaming tiles, threaded background asset streaming (`ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request`), 3-tier distance LOD, national highway network (NH-1, NH-2, NH-3), regional ambient crossfades, and interactive fullscreen world map (`[M]`).
8. **Visual Standards & Dynamic Weather Engine**: PBR asphalt road with procedural FBM puddle accumulation and rain ripples, Gerstner wave river/ocean water with depth absorption, wind foliage sway with subsurface scattering, 4-split CSM directional shadows, SSAO, SSR, volumetric fog, dynamic 24-hour sun/moon trajectory, automated civic streetlighting, and 5 weather conditions (Sunny, Cloudy, Monsoon Rain, River Fog, Kalbaishakhi Thunderstorm).
9. **Final Game Systems & User Interface**: Bootable Main Menu with original theme music, in-game Pause Menu (`[ESC]`), multi-slot Save/Load persistence (Slots 1-3 + Autosave), Settings modal with graphics/audio/controls configuration, Mission Log & Dossier (`[J]`), corner circular tactical Mini-map radar, and Windows Desktop Forward+ release preset.
